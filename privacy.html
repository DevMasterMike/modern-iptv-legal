Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version

We run no servers and track no one. Your information stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. The app is free and sells nothing, so it handles no payments.

Who we are

Modern IPTV is made by Michael Berinshteyn, an individual developer in Erie, Pennsylvania, USA. This policy covers the app on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

Sign in with Apple

Apple gives the app a stable identifier, plus your name and email only if you choose to share them. They stay on your devices and in your private iCloud, and we never receive them.

Your TV service login

Your provider's server address, username and password are kept in your device's Keychain. They sync to your other Apple devices through encrypted fields in your private iCloud database, which we can't access.

Your library

Favorites, watch progress, the teams you follow and your show reminders sync the same way. Downloads stay on the device where you made them, and each device makes its own notifications.

Services the app contacts

Your device connects directly to your TV service (your login and what you watch), TMDB (trending titles and artwork, with your language and region), Wikipedia and Wikidata (cast photos and biographies), ESPN (game schedules, scores and team logos), TVmaze (when a show you follow next airs, by the show's name), GitHub Pages (when you open a shared link) and Apple (iCloud, Sign in with Apple and Siri). Each one sees your IP address and your request under its own privacy policy, and we never send them your name or email.

Your Apple Account email

The app keeps your Sign in with Apple email in your private iCloud, so an Apple TV on your Apple Account knows it's you.

Ask about a title

Answers come from Apple Intelligence running on your device. Your questions and the answers never reach us.

No tracking, ads or analytics

The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't use it for targeted ads or profiling.

How long it's kept

Your information stays until you delete it or your account. The services above keep their own logs under their own policies.

Deleting everything

Settings has a Delete Account button that erases your iCloud records, downloads, TV service login and every local file. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for this app in your device's Settings, under your Apple Account.

Children

Modern IPTV is for people 16 and older and isn't directed to children under 13. If you believe a child's information is in the app, email us and we'll help delete it.

Security

Your login is protected by the Keychain and iCloud's encryption, though no system is perfect. If a breach ever affects your personal information, we'll notify you and the authorities as Pennsylvania law and other applicable laws require.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, and to opt out of its sale, targeted advertising or profiling. We do none of those things, and you can email us to use any right without being treated differently.

Changes

When this policy changes we'll update the effective date above, and we'll tell you in the app about important changes.

Contact

Questions and requests go to berinshteyn@gmail.com. We answer every one.
