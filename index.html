What Modern IPTV is

Modern IPTV is a player for the IPTV service you already subscribe to. It doesn't sell, host or include any channels, movies or shows. Sign in with the server address, username and password your provider gave you, and the app shows your live channels with a guide, your movies and series with artwork and details, and your teams' games.

Signing in on Apple TV

Open Modern IPTV on your Apple TV and it shows a code. On your iPhone, open Modern IPTV, go to Settings › Sign in on Apple TV, and point the camera at the TV. Your login and library are sent to the TV through your iCloud, encrypted for that TV only. If your Apple TV and iPhone use the same Apple Account, the TV signs in on its own.

Membership

The first year is free, then $1.00 a year (or the local equivalent), billed by Apple. It covers Apple's yearly developer fee and nothing else. Manage or cancel it in Settings › Membership in the app, or in your Apple Account's Subscriptions settings. You keep access until the period you paid for ends.

Delete your account or data

In the app: Settings › Delete Account erases your iCloud records, downloads, TV service login and every local file. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for this app under Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple on your device.
